Rules and policies

These are the same at both courses unless the table says otherwise.

When new days open

WhoHow far ahead
2 or more players, 18 holes4 days, counting today
Everyone else3 days, counting today

Each new day opens at 9 PM.

Why you might not see tee times

  • The day isn't open yet for your group size.
  • Every time that matches your filters is booked.
  • It's past the last tee time of the day.

The tee time list always tells you which one it is.

Last tee time of the day

The list ends 100 minutes before sunset so every group can finish before dark.
